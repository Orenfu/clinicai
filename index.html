The problem, as every ACO already reports it

Shortage → access gap → missed diagnoses and ER overuse → higher spend. It's one chain, and the tools on the market today only ever touch one link of it.

Telehealth · K Health, Teladoc

Can't scale beyond physician supply

Every visit still consumes a clinician hour. The shortage that limits the clinic limits the video call too — access grows only as fast as hiring does.

Chatbots · Ada Health, Infermedica

Can't observe the patient

Symptom checkers triage from typed text. They can't see, hear, or examine — so they escalate defensively and add work instead of absorbing it.

Ambient scribes · Nuance, Abridge

Document but don't diagnose

Saves minutes of paperwork per encounter, delivers no new capacity — the physician still runs every visit and makes every call.

Primary care doesn't need a faster chatbot or a better scribe. It needs a clinician-shaped technology.

One platform, eight capabilities, built on a partnership with a frontier AI model maker (confidential) — observing the patient, reasoning like a clinician, and completing the visit end to end.

Multimodal assessment

Body language, facial expression, pain, voice & respiratory biomarkers, mood, skin screening, rPPG vitals.

Clinical knowledge

Evidence-based medicine and current practice guidelines, applied through dynamic clinical algorithms.

Medical reasoning

Clinical guardrails, differential diagnosis, and a working hypothesis for every patient, every visit.
